Sixtytwo

A growing number of companies are training their own models. Data control and GPU economics are pushing companies to build their own clusters. But running your own GPUs means taking on everything a cloud provider does invisibly with none of their tooling. The same is true for teams renting from neoclouds, where the hardware is someone else's but the reliability is still yours.

Sixtytwo validates GPU cluster health before, between, and during training runs so that bad nodes are caught before they take down a job. Over time, the data we collect across clusters will let us predict which nodes are likely to fail before they do.

Cloud providers handle all of this invisibly. Everyone else is left building it themselves with scripts and manual processes. Sixtytwo gives them the health layer that cloud training takes for granted.

The future of AI runs on-prem.
